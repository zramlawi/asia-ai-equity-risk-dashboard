# Bubble-risk methodology

The dashboard keeps price behavior separate from reported fundamentals, liquidity, and country macro context. A price-risk signal alone is not evidence of a bubble and should not be combined into a single opaque recommendation.

## Price-risk module

The Price Risk tab uses Yahoo Finance adjusted close history to display price trajectory, maximum drawdown, and realized annualized volatility. These are backward-looking measures of market behavior, not forecasts.

## Fundamental and liquidity modules

The Fundamentals & Liquidity tab separately presents reported valuation, profitability, leverage/liquidity inputs, field coverage, and two transparent 0–100 scores:

- Fundamental Stretch measures high valuation multiples and comparatively weak reported profitability or growth according to the published normalization ranges.
- Liquidity Risk measures lower reported average daily dollar volume and weaker current/quick ratios.

Missing inputs are excluded, not fabricated, and a score must be interpreted with its coverage percentage.

## Macro module

The Country Macro tab uses World Bank annual data for growth, inflation, unemployment, current-account balance, and official exchange-rate context. The module exposes source dates because indicators can have different latest observation years. It is context only and does not score geopolitical events.

## Interpretation

A high price-risk observation alongside low fundamental coverage is an evidence-quality warning rather than confirmation of a bubble. Likewise, a high stretch score does not establish valuation error, and macro data is too lagged for real-time trading use. The application deliberately labels analyst estimates, options, short interest, institutional ownership, and live geopolitical scoring as provider-required/unavailable.

This is research software, not investment advice.
